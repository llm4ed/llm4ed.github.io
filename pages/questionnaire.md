<div class="ai4ed-section-intro">
  <p>We are inviting emergency department physicians and nurses to share what information is most useful in a past medical history summary and how that information should be presented in practice.</p>
</div>

<div class="ai4ed-feature-grid ai4ed-feature-grid--split">
  <article class="ai4ed-feature-card">

    <p>Emergency Department physicians and nurses who use ConnectCare and regularly review past medical history during acute care workflows.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="ai4ed-feature-card">
    <h3>What we are studying</h3>
    <p>We want to understand which information is most vital in a past medical history summary and how it should be presented to support real clinical use.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="ai4ed-feature-card">
    <h3>What to expect</h3>

  </article>
</div>
