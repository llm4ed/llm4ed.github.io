<div class="ai4ed-section-intro">
  <p>The questionnaire is designed to help the AI4ED team understand what information emergency department clinicians need in a past medical history summary and how that information should be presented in practice.</p>
  <p>Public access is not open at this time. Participation is limited to eligible and approved participants who receive access through the project team.</p>
</div>

<div class="ai4ed-feature-grid ai4ed-feature-grid--split">

  </article>
  <article class="ai4ed-feature-card">
    <h2>What we are studying</h2>
    <p>We want to understand which information is most vital in a past medical history summary and how it should be presented to support real clinical use.</p>
