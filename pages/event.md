## Emergency Department Questionnaire Event

Are you an Emergency Department (ED) physician or nurse? Have you ever found yourself clicking through dozens of tabs on ConnectCare to find your patients' past medical history information?

We are running a short research questionnaire to understand what information is most vital in a past medical history summary and the best way to present this information to you. Your input will inform the development of an AI-enabled auto-summary tool.

The anonymous survey can be started directly by clicking Open Questionnaire. The survey takes less than 10 minutes. As a thank-you, you can enter a draw to win a $20 gift card.
